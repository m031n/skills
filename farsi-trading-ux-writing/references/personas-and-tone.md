# Context, persona, and tone

Choose the closest persona and adapt vocabulary without changing the product's truth.

| Persona | Typical context | Tone | Copy emphasis |
|---|---|---|---|
| New or occasional investor | onboarding, first deposit, first order | reassuring, plain, patient | define the action and the immediate consequence |
| Active trader | quote, order ticket, edit/cancel, execution status | concise, fast, precise | status, price/quantity context, and the next action |
| Portfolio holder | holdings, P&L, dividends, history | calm, factual, legible | timeframe, units, ownership, and what changed |
| Risk-sensitive user | leverage, volatility, rejection, withdrawal, irreversible actions | transparent, cautious, respectful | material risk, exact consequence, recovery or exit |
| Support or operations user | verification, limits, failed transfer, review state | professional, explanatory | known reason, expected wait only when supplied, and resolution path |

Default voice is clear semi-formal Persian: professional without bureaucratic stiffness, warm without cheerleading, and confident only where the product has evidence. The tone may become more concise under time pressure and more explicit as financial risk rises.

### Context calibration

- Before an action: explain what the action will do and expose material constraints.
- During an action: show the real current state; do not imply completion while pending.
- After success: confirm the result and provide a useful follow-up only if one exists.
- After failure: name the known problem and give the safest practical recovery.
- For irreversible or high-stakes actions: use exact nouns, quantities, side (buy/sell), instrument, and consequence. Avoid jokes, urgency, pressure, or euphemism.

Do not write investment recommendations, fabricated certainty, or unsupported claims such as «این تغییر سود شما را افزایش می‌دهد». Use «هدف این تغییر...» when it is only a product goal.
